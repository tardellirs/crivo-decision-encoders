**BTZSC caveat (zs-v2 rows).** The MoritzLaurer `zeroshot-v2.0` checkpoints we ran (DeBERTa-v3-large, ModernBERT-large; non-commercial variants) were fine-tuned on training splits of **all 22 BTZSC datasets** (up to 500 examples per class) with BTZSC's exact label verbalizers, and a few BTZSC test items are in their training data (yelpreviews 4.5%, agnews 1.7%; trueteacher shares the source article for 6.1%). Their BTZSC scores are therefore supervised few-shot, not zero-shot: report them as an **upper reference, not a like-for-like zero-shot baseline**. On BTZSC unseen-4 the exposure is asymmetric in their favour (unseen by Crivo v2, seen by zs-v2). Evidence: `btzsc/zsv2_overlap.md`. Overlap between zs-v2's training datasets and S1-Arena tasks was not audited.
